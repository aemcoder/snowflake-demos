What is work order software?

Work order software is designed to help businesses organize and manage requests for tasks such as repairs, maintenance, or service delivery. Instead of juggling spreadsheets, emails, or sticky notes, teams can keep all work orders in one centralized, digital platform–making it easy to assign tasks, set priorities, and track progress in real time.

With Knack, you’re not locked into a one-size-fits-all system. Our platform lets you create a custom online work order system tailored to your exact workflow–without writing a single line of code. That means you get the features you need, skip the ones you don’t, and keep your process running smoothly from request to completion.
