How Experience Platform Agent Orchestrator works.

Learn how the four core components of Agent Orchestrator work together to ensure that Adobe Experience Platform Agents can execute complex tasks.

Conversational interface.

Adobe Experience Platform Agents are accessed and prompted to take action via our conversational interface, AI Assistant.

Reasoning engine.

The reasoning engine interprets prompts and determines the necessary steps to accomplish a user's objectives, while taking specified constraints into account.

Knowledge base.

The knowledge base provides essential context for the reasoning engine, so agents assign the right tasks to the right resources. It helps agents interpret requests accurately, support role-specific prompts, and prioritize key skill requirements.

Questions? We have answers.

What is an AI agent? +
AI agents are intelligent operators that can understand user intent, interpret goals, create plans, and take action — either independently or alongside people. Agents can work autonomously or semi-autonomously, using skills like planning or reasoning and interacting with tools. They also utilize memory, drawing on experience and behavioral data to enhance their performance.
What are Adobe Experience Platform Agents? +
Adobe Experience Platform Agents are a new class of enterprise AI designed to work alongside marketing and creative teams. Purpose-built to support customer experience orchestration, these agents are focused on automating high-value tasks, surfacing actionable insights, and creating capacity for individual contributors so marketers and creatives can spend more time on strategic work.
What are agentic skills? +
Agentic skills are the abilities that let an AI agent act independently and make decisions guided by context. These skills allow the agent to take purposeful actions that are guided by context and aimed at achieving specific objectives.
Does an Adobe Experience Platform Agent require a user prompt, or can it complete tasks autonomously? +
Adobe Experience Platform Agents can operate both autonomously and semi-autonomously. When proactively providing insights and recommendations, the agents act on their own. In other cases — such as when co-ideating with a user — they seek user input before taking further action, acting semi-autonomously. Regardless of the mode, Experience Platform Agents will always act within the guidelines and limitations set by the user and only at the user's direction.
What is the user experience when engaging with Adobe Experience Platform Agents? +

Adobe Experience Platform Agents can be accessed through a conversational interface — such as AI Assistant in Adobe Experience Platform — where a user can submit prompts in natural language to complete tasks. In the background, the reasoning engine steps in. It looks at the prompt, assesses what the task is, and creates a plan to complete the task. This plan may involve one or more expert AI agents, each specialized in specific skills required to execute the steps necessary for task completion, such as pulling data, generating insights, creating content, and more. These agents also tap into the knowledge base to gather any information they need to complete an objective.

Throughout the process, the Agent Orchestrator ensures that the agents' work can be checked, validated, and is repeatable, so you get reliable results every time.

What kind of data is being used by Adobe Experience Platform Agents? +
The reasoning engine serves as the "brain" behind Adobe Experience Platform Agents. It uses large language models (LLMs) and a comprehensive knowledge base to generate responses and carry out tasks. To train these LLMs, Adobe relies on synthetic data — like sample prompts created by Adobe solutions and engineering teams — so the models can learn effectively. Adobe does not use customer prompts, customer data, metadata, or outputs for training the LLMs. Plus, we ensure that third-party LLM providers are not allowed to use Adobe customer data for their own training or manual review.
How are Adobe Experience Platform Agents different from other AI agents? +

Adobe Experience Platform Agents are specifically designed to support workflows and tasks that deliver exceptional customer experience. Here's what sets them apart:

Grounded in real-time customer experience data and content: Agents are powered by Adobe Experience Platform and leverage its semantic understanding of journeys, content, behavior, and performance signals. Their actions are context-aware and aligned with business goals.

Designed for orchestration at scale: Agents do not operate in silos. Through Adobe Experience Platform Agent Orchestrator, they coordinate with other Adobe agents or third-party agents across workflows, applications, and systems to deliver results in real time.

Extensible by design: The agent framework supports a wide range of use cases. Customers, partners, and developers can build, configure, or integrate agents to match the specific needs of their teams and industries.
